Welcome!

This blog is where I document my journey through cybersecurity.

There are countless articles online that explain *what* a vulnerability is or *how* to solve a challenge. My goal isn't to repeat them. Instead, I want to document my own research, experiments, failures, and lessons learned while exploring computer security.

Most posts here will come from firsthand work: solving CTF challenges, building security tools, reading research papers, experimenting with new techniques, or analyzing real-world security problems. Whenever possible, I'll explain not only **what** worked, but also **why** it worked and the reasoning that led me there.

## What You Can Expect

Topics on this blog will include:

* Application Security
* AI Security
* Security Research
* CTF Writeups
* Reverse Engineering
* Digital Forensics
* Exploit Development
* Security Tooling
* Experiments and Research Notes

Not every post will fit neatly into one category. Cybersecurity is an interconnected field, and many problems span multiple disciplines.

## Writing Philosophy

I value technical depth over volume.

Rather than publishing frequently, I'd rather publish something that is accurate, reproducible, and genuinely useful. If a post is here, it's because I found the topic worth investigating and documenting.

This blog is primarily a knowledge base for my future self, a place to record ideas, discoveries, and solutions so I can revisit them later. If others find these notes useful as well, that's a welcome bonus.

## Looking Ahead

I'm continually learning, building, and exploring new areas of security. As my interests evolve, so will this blog.

Whether it's a CTF writeup, a deep dive into an application vulnerability, an AI security experiment, or a tool I've built, every post represents another step in understanding how systems work, how they fail, and how they can be made more secure.

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you find something here worth reading.

Plus Ultra.
